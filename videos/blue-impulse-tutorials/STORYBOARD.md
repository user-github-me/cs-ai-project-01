# Blue Impulse teaching storyboard

## Video direction

Use actual book excerpts and page numbers throughout. Each lesson follows the user’s question → objectives → welcome → analogy → demonstration → recap → original-cover credit sequence. Anticipate each spatial movement for three frames. Preserve a quiet reading hold for book excerpts, then reveal interpretation with the narration. The numerical demonstrations are dedicated frames, assigned one per frame worker. Shared lesson layouts are generated from the locked content data. Narration times, rather than estimates, determine final durations.

The plan is approved by the request for finished MP4 files; a heads-up was posted before building. No separate preview approval is required.
