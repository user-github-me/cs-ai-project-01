# Tutorial output verification

All five delivered MP4s passed the checks recorded in [final-video-checks.json](final-video-checks.json). The [readable report](final-video-checks.md) explains their scope. The videos are 600 seconds each, 1920×1080 at 60 fps, and below 250 MB.

Checks cover all 180,000 decoded video frames, exact copied audio, 489 caption cues, chapter references, loop endpoints, and ten AI-assisted inspections of final encoded demonstration/caption frames. The encoded/ folders contain compact proof images. Document and citation checks are included separately.

The preflight records summarize all 5,750 beat samples and deterministic frame checks performed before encoding. Production HTML, render scripts and large intermediate files are kept locally at the user's request; those source hashes are identifiers of checked local inputs, not links to required downloads.

These records do not claim a teammate's complete viewing, a beginner's attempt, a human listening test, classroom rehearsal, or course-platform submission. Those required human steps remain to be documented by the people involved.
