# Chapter 6 — Strengths & Limitations

## Learning outcomes

Evaluate physical and network constraints; distinguish scalability, interoperability and resilience; compare predictive analytics with behavioural modelling; assess resource allocation, explainability, bias and responsible deployment.

By the end, explain the chapter’s mechanisms, apply them to the mission, and justify a design decision using its constraints. Check learning by completing the application checkpoints and exercises without first reading feedback.
