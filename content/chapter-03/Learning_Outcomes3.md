# Chapter 3 — Hardware

## Learning outcomes

Classify hardware; compare memory and storage; explain processors, pipelining, parallelism and interrupts; connect sensors, converters and actuators; reason with logic gates; evaluate embedded systems, interfaces and AI accelerators.

By the end, explain the chapter’s mechanisms, apply them to the mission, and justify a design decision using its constraints. Check learning by completing the application checkpoints and exercises without first reading feedback.
