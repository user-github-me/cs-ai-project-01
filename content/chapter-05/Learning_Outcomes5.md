# Chapter 5 — Applications

## Learning outcomes

You will:
-	Discover how hardware, networks, and behaviorist AI are used in real-world applications.
-	Understand how physical devices, communication systems, and intelligent algorithms work together.
-	Explain how AI systems respond to feedback and adapt their behavior to achieve specific goals.
-	Be able to explain how AI systems respond to feedback and adapt their behavior to achieve specific goals.
-	Recognize the opportunities and challenges of integrating these technologies into everyday applications.
