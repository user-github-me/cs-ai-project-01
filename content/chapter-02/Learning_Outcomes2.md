# Chapter 2 — Behaviorism & Behaviorist AI

## Learning outcomes

Distinguish classical and operant conditioning; identify stimulus–response rules and layered robotics; explain reinforcement-learning components, policies, values and exploration; interpret a Q update and the parts of an MDP.

By the end, explain the chapter’s mechanisms, apply them to the mission, and justify a design decision using its constraints. Check learning by completing the application checkpoints and exercises without first reading feedback.
