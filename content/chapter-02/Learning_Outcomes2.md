## Chapter 2: Behaviorism and Behaviorist AI:
You will learn about:
- What behaviorism is and its main principles.
- How behavior is influenced by external stimuli and environmental interactions.
- How observable responses to stimuli explain learning and behavioral changes.
- What behavior-based robotics is and how robots use sensory input to respond to their surroundings.
- What Behaviorist AI is and how it relates to the principles of behaviorism.
- How AI can develop intelligent behavior through sensory input and interaction with the environment rather than relying on complex internal models of the world.
- What Reinforcement Learning (RL) is and how agents learn through trial and error using rewards and penalties.
- The basic components of Reinforcement Learning, including agents, environments, states, actions, and rewards.
- The common algorithms and techniques used in Reinforcement Learning.
- What Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) are and their role in modeling decision-making under uncertainty.
- How Reinforcement Learning and MDPs enable AI systems to learn, make decisions, and adapt their behavior to different environments.
