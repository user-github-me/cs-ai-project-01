You're the computer expert of the team, and you know exactly how to convert Binary into ordinary text.


The password is right in front of you:

00100000
01001001 01010011
00100000
01010011 01001111
00100000
01000011 01001111 01001111 01001100


Your Mission!

4. Combine all the characters to reveal the secret password!
